1. Introduction

Video-based task learning is widespread, but not personalized.

  • Video shows processes, but a fixed viewpoint limits tacit knowledge.
  • Learners turn to comments to fill this gap.
  • Comments support:
  • Questions
  • Goals
  • Experience
  • Explanations

However… the role of comments in the learning process is under-explored.

We look at three stages of learning from a video:

  1. Before: search
  2. During: watch
  3. After: reflect

2. Exploratory study

Methodology

  • Semi-structured interviews (about 30 minutes each, 14 participants)
  • Co-watching sessions (about 30 minutes each, 8 participants)
  • Analyzed using thematic analysis

3. Findings: the role of comments across learning stages

Before: trust and video selection

  • Comments act as trust signals for video selection.
  • Comments reduce uncertainty before learning starts.

During: sensemaking and personalization

  • Enable collaborative understanding and adaptation.
  • Transform passive watching into active learning.

After: reflection and extension

  • Comments support reflection and deeper learning.
  • Comments extend learning beyond the video.

Surfaced challenges

  • Difficult comment discovery
  • Disrupted task flow
  • Limited access

4. Design opportunities

Transform comments from passive content into an integrated, stage-aware learning support system.

Support informed video selection

Surface collective signals from comments.

Enable in-situ sensemaking

Embed step-based insights directly into video playback.

Reflection and knowledge extension

Structure feedback by step for effortless navigation and analysis.

Improve access and navigation

Minimize manual navigation and noise to enable seamless, hands-free mobile access.

5. Limitation and future work

Research should explore broader demographics and diverse platforms to better decode the underlying motivations for social commenting.
